# Privacy Policy for Markdown Preview

**Last Updated: February 5, 2026**

## Introduction

Welcome to Markdown Preview. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

**Key Point: Markdown Preview does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All data remains on your device.**

## Information We Do NOT Collect

Markdown Preview is designed with privacy in mind. We do **NOT** collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or statistics
- Browsing history or app usage patterns
- Any data for advertising purposes
- Financial or payment information

## Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores minimal data locally on your device to enhance your user experience:

### Theme Preferences
- **What**: Your selected theme preference (dark or light mode)
- **Where**: Stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences
- **Purpose**: To remember your preferred theme when you reopen the app
- **Sharing**: This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted anywhere

### File Content
- **What**: Markdown or text files you choose to import into the app
- **Where**: Processed in memory on your device; files are read from your device storage
- **Purpose**: To display and preview markdown content
- **Sharing**: File content is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with any external service

## Permissions

Markdown Preview requests the following permissions:

### Storage/Media Access
- **Purpose**: To allow you to import markdown (.md) and text (.txt) files from your device
- **Usage**: The app only reads files that you explicitly select through the file picker
- **Scope**: We do not scan, index, or access any files without your direct action

The app does **NOT** require or use:
- Internet access
- Camera access
- Microphone access
- Contact access
- Location access

## Data Security

Since all data processing occurs locally on your device and no data is transmitted over the internet, your information remains secure under your device's own security measures. We recommend:

- Keeping your device's operating system updated
- Using device lock features (PIN, pattern, biometric)
- Only importing files from trusted sources

## Third-Party Services

Markdown Preview does **NOT** integrate with any third-party services, including:

- Analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Social media platforms
- Cloud storage services
- Crash reporting services

## Children's Privacy

Markdown Preview does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages as it operates entirely offline and does not collect or transmit any data.

## Data Retention

- **Theme preferences**: Stored until you uninstall the app or clear the app's data
- **File content**: Only held in memory while the app is active; not permanently stored by the app

To delete all locally stored preferences, you can:
1. Go to your device's Settings
2. Navigate to Apps > Markdown Preview
3. Select "Clear Data" or "Clear Storage"

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Since we do not collect any contact information, we cannot notify you directly of changes. Please check this policy occasionally for updates.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, traditional data subject rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) do not apply in the conventional sense. However, you maintain full control over:

- Your theme preferences (changeable in-app or by clearing app data)
- Which files you import (you choose each file individually)
- App installation (you can uninstall at any time, removing all associated data)

## Open Source Libraries

Markdown Preview uses the following open-source libraries:
- Flutter framework (by Google)
- markdown_widget (for rendering markdown)
- file_picker (for file selection)
- shared_preferences (for local preference storage)

These libraries process data locally on your device and do not transmit information externally.
